UNDER THE SURFACE

How it works.

A transparent look at the machine learning and decision rules behind your readout.

THE IDEA

Emotion isn’t just a sentence.

Still brings your reflection together with the context around it, then offers one manageable next step. The model can be uncertain; your own perspective always matters more.

✳WORDSCONTEXT
01

Read the reflection

The model looks at word patterns in your note. Short notes are still welcome, though they can be harder to read confidently.

TEXT FEATURES
02

Add the context

Sleep, energy, stress, session details and time of day give the model more than words alone.

HYBRID MODEL
03

Estimate state & intensity

Two classifiers trained on the project’s supplied reflections estimate an emotional state and a 1–5 intensity, with a confidence score.

NAIVE BAYES
04

Suggest a gentle action

A transparent decision layer combines the prediction with energy and time of day to suggest what to try and when.

DECISION RULES
✳
What this model can and can’t do

It learns patterns from a labeled dataset and offers a reflective prompt. It can misread ambiguity, culture, or context, and it cannot diagnose or replace professional care.
